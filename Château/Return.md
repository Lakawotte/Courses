# 1. Theory
The **return** is simply the money made or lost after an investment over some period of time. To best describe a **return**, we need to take in account **the three T** :
-  Taxes
- Time
- Transaction cost
We can also add **cost of opportunity** and **inflation** to the list of parameters we want to take in account when calculation our **overall return**. Most of the time, **returns** are annualized. 

# 2. Interpretation
### I
A **nominal return** is the unchanged amount of money in currencies that you've earn throughout a period of time. To calculate it, we just substract the money we had invested to the money we get at the end of the time period, and we also add all the distributions and substract all the outlays if we had any.
### II
A **real return** is the **nominal return** with **inflation** and others factors that fluctuates over time. It allows us to determine the true money from our investment and is commonly used before investing to determine how much we'll get.
### III
There is different ratios involving **return** :
	-[[Return On Investment]] **ROI**
	-[[Return On Equity]] **ROE**
	-[[Return On Assets]] **ROA**
	-[[Return On Capital]] **ROC**
