# Definition
## I. Idea
Jon Andersen wanted an indicator of the **[[Volatility|volatility]]** around the *tendancy*, which acts like a **[[Confidence Interval|confidence interval]]**.

They are very similar to the **[[Bollinger Bands|Bollinger bands]]** but their are interpreted in a slightly different way. Indeed to ones from Bollinger are made out of the *mobile mean* and the **[[Standard Deviation|standard deviation]]** and the **standard error brands** are based on the **[[Standard Error|standard error]]**.

Before the rise of computers, *tendancy* lines were drawn by hand on paper, but now one can use powerfull machines to make multi-parameters technical analysis. Despite the complexity and accuracy of the softwares, such indicators were still hard to incorporate in security analysis. This is what drived Andersen.
