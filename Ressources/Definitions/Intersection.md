# Definition
## I. Statement

>[!tip] Intersection
Let $A$ and $B$ be two **[[Subset|subsets]]** of a finite **[[Set|set]]** $\mathcal{E}$. We call intersection of $A$ and $B$ the set made of elements from $\mathcal{E}$ which are both in $A$ and in $B$
>$$
A\cap B=\{e\in\mathcal{E},e\in A\vee e\in B\}
>$$
