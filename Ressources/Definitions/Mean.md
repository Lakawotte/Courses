# Definition
## I. Statement

>[!hint] Definition 1 : Arithmetic Mean
$$
\bar{X}=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=0}^n X_{i}
$$

>[tip] Definition 2 : Geometric Mean
Let denote the increases by $u_i$
$$
\bar{X}=(\prod^n_{k=1}1+u_{k})^{1/n}-1
$$
#### Note :
In most cases, we add $-1$ to get the overall change as a percentage rather than as a compounded factor.

### 2. Geometric Mean
The geometric mean is used mainly to compare annual returns. To get the growth rate, we ubstitute the values $u_n$ by the different increases and decreases of the market and it will give us the return of all the period merged together.
It's also used in the **[[CAGR]]** (Compounded Annual Growth Rate) or the **[[Rate Of Return|RoR]]** (Rate of Return).
Sometimes it can be difficult to know how much we gained during a period where we added or substracted money from the portfolio, this is why we use this mean.

# Example
Let's say that the first year, we entered with a $1000$$ portfolio. During the year, the market increased by $13.4\%$ and after corrected by $41.15\%$.
The annual return is given by the geometric mean and gives us an overall $-18.31\%$ increase.
