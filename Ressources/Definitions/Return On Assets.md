# Definition
## I. Statement

>[!hint] Definition 1 : Ratio
$$
ROA=\frac{I}{A}
$$
Where $I$ is the *net income* and $A$ the *total assets*.

>[!tip] Definition 2 : DuPont framework
$$
ROA=NPM\times AT
$$
#### Note :
As in the [[Return On Equity]], we can brake down the formula using the **DuPont framework**.

# Application
## I. Meaning
The **return of assets** is a measurement of the capacity of the company to generate profits from assets. It shows how much net income is earned per dollar of assets.
It tells us about the efficiency of the company to made profits from assets. When it's high, it means that the company is generating more profits relative to its assets base.
One key advantage compared to the [[Return On Equity]] is that it takes debt into account.
## II. Use
At the end of the day, its important to remember that we can't compare **ROA**s from vastly different industries due to different assets structures.
