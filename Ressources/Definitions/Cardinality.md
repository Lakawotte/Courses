# Example
## I. Pentagon
In a pentagon, let denote $\text{card}(\mathcal{E}_{n})$ the number of points of the $n$th pentagon :
$$
\begin{split}
\text{card}(\mathcal{E}_{n})&=\text{card}(\mathcal{E}_{1})+\sum_{i=2}^n\text{card}(\mathcal{E}_{i})-\text{card}(\mathcal{E}_{i-1})\\
&=1+\sum_{i=2}^n 5i-5\\
&=\frac{5}{2}n(n-1)+1\\
\end{split}
$$
