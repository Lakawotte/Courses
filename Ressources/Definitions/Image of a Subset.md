>[!hint] Definition

f(A)=\{f(x)|x\in A\}=\{y\in Y\}

# Application
## I. Meaning
>The **direct image** of a **[[Subset|subset]]** $A$ from $X$ by an **[[Application|application]]** $f:X\to Y$ is the **[[Subset|subset]]** from $Y$ formed by the elements, which by $f$ has at least one *preimage* in $A$.
