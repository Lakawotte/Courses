# Definition
## I. Statement

>[!hint] Definition
>Let $\mathcal{E}$ be finite **[[Set|set]]** and $p>0$. A **p-tuple** or **p-element list** of elements from $\mathcal{E}$ is an ordered list of elements from $\mathcal{E}$ not necesseraly distincts. The **[[Set|set]]** of the **p-tuples** of $\mathcal{E}$ is the **[[Cartesian Product|cartesian product]]** of $p$ copies of $\mathcal{E}$, which is denoted $\mathcal{E}^p$
>$$
>\mathcal{E}^p=\{(e_{i})_{1\le i\le p},e_{i}\in\mathcal{E}^p\}
>$$
## II. Extensions
### 1. Properties

>[!tldr] **[[Cardinality]]** of a **p-tuple**
>$$
>\text{card}(\mathcal{E}^p)=\text{card}(\mathcal{E})^p
>$$
