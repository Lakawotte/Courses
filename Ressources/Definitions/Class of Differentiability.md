## I. Statement

>[!hint] Definition
>Let $I\subset\mathbb{R}$ be an interval and $f:I\to\mathbb{R}$ a real-valuated function. We say that $f$ is $n$ times **[[Differentiability|differentiable]]** and we write $f^{(n)}$. It is a definition by induction, since
>$$
>f^{(n+1)}=(f^{(n)})'
>$$
