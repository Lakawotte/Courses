# Definition
## I. Statement

>[!hint] Definition 1 : Two Variables independent of Each Other
>Random variables $X_i,i\in\mathbb{N}$ are said to be independent of each other if, for all $(i,j)\left[\!\left[0;n\right]\!\right]^2$, and for all $(x_i,x_j)$ taken respectively by $X_i$ and  $X_j$ :
$$
P((X_{i},X_{j})=(x_{i},x_{j}))=P(X_{i}=x_{i})\times P(X_{j}=x_{j})
$$
