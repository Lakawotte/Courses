# Definition
## I. Statement

>[!hint] Definition
$$
ROI=\frac{V_{f}-V_{0}}{V_{0}}
$$
Where $V_f$ is the *final value* of the investment, and $V_0$ is the *original value* of it.

>[!tip] Definition 2 : **[[Equity]]**
$$
ROI=TD+E-NOA
$$
Where $TD$ is the *total debt*, $E$ is the **[[Equity]]** and $NOA$ is the *Non-Operating Assets*.

# Application
## I. Meaning
The **ROI** is a rudimentary measurement of the **profitability** of an orignial investment.

The **ROI** is the scalar which multiplied to the original value gives the money earned (resp. lost) over a certain period of time.
A major issue of it is that it doesn't take time in account, so we might lose information about **[[cost of opportunity]]**.
