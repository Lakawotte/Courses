# Application
## I. Meaning

$\phi$ is *linear* if and only if there is $A\in\mathcal{M}_{n,p}(\mathbb{R})$ such that for all vector $x\in E$, the left product by $A$ of the column of its coordinates in $\mathfrak{B}$ is identical to the column of the coordinates of $\phi(x)$ in $\mathfrak{C}$.
