Where the $EBIT$ stands for *Earnings Before Interest And Taxes* and the $TR$ is the *tax rate*.

# Application
## I. Meaning
The **NOPA** represents the profit made by a company after accounting for taxesbut before any other **financing costs**.
This is, the **NOPA** calculates only the performance of the business, regardless of how it's financed. More precisely, this measurement only focus on the core operations of the company, and don't take in count any tax benefits or one-time gains since they're not considered as regular operations.
## II. Use
# Example
For instance, a company had an operating income of 857 900 $, with a tax rate of $28\%$. The net operating profit after tax is 617 668 $.
