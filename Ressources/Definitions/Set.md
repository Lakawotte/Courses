>[!tldr] Complementary
>Let $\mathcal{E}$ be a finite **set**. The complementary of a **[[Subset|subset]]** $A$ from $\mathcal{E}$ is the **set** of elements that are not in $A$.
>$$
\bar{A}=\{e\in\mathcal{E},e\notin A\}
>$$

>[!tldr] Morgan Rule
>$$
\bar{A\cup B}=\bar{A}\cap\bar{B}
>$$
