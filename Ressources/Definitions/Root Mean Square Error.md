# Definition
## I. Statement

>[!hint] Definition
>Let $X$ be a *random variable* and $\hat{X}$ an *estimator*. Then
>$$
\begin{split}
RMSE(\hat{X})&=\sqrt{ MSE(\hat{X})}\\
&=\frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}\|\hat{X}-X\|_{2}\\
\end{split}
>$$
