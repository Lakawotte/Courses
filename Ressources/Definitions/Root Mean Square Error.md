# Definition
## I. Statement

>[!hint] Definition
>Let $(X_{i})_{i\in I}$ be a *sample* with *true mean value* $\hat{X}$. Then
>$$
\begin{split}
RMSE(X)&=\sqrt{ MSE(X)}\\
&=\frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}\|\hat{X}-X\|_{2}\\
\end{split}
>$$
