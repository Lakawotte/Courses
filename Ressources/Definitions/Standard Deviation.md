# Application
## I. Meaning
The **standard deviation** is the measurement of the avergage distance between the datas and the mean of a set. The further the points are from the mean, the higher the standard deviation will be. It's always positive and symmetrical.
## II. Use
# Example
For example, the market evolved during the year and we get these values : $1.2\%$, $-0.3\%$, $0.65\%$ and $0.28\%$. The mean is $0.46\%$ and the standard deviation is equal to $0.55\%$, which means that the points are approximately distants of $0.55\%$ from the mean.
