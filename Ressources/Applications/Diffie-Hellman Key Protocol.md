# Definition
## I. Statement

>[!hint] Construction for $2$ users
>Both users agree on a number $n\in\mathbb{N}$ and on a *finite cyclic group* $\mathbb{F}_n$. They then generate $g\in\mathbb{F}_n$ which is supposed to be know by attackers.
>1. Alice chose $1<a<n$ in $\mathbb{F}_n$ and compute $g^a$, which is sent to Bob
>2. Bob chose $1<b<n$ in $\mathbb{F}_n$ and compute $g^b$, which is sent to Bob
>3. After the exchange, they again compute : Alice makes $(g^{a})^b)$ and Bob makes $(g^{b})^a)$
