### A. Definition

>[!tip] On-Balance Volume
>Let $\mathrm{OBV}$ be the *on-balance volume* and $t$ a date. Then the *on-balacne volume* is a *sequence* over $t$ defined by recursion :
>$$
\mathrm{OBV}_{t}=\mathrm{OBV}_{t-1}+\begin{cases}V\,\,\mathrm{if}\,\,C_{t}>C_{t-1}\\\\end{cases}
>$$
