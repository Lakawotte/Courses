# Formula
## I. Statement
### 1. Expression

>[!hint] Formula
>Let $E$ and $F$ be two *vector spaces* of dimension $n$. Then for $f\in\mathcal{L}(E,F)$ we have
>$$
\mathrm{rg}(f)+\mathrm{dim}\,\mathrm{Ker}(f)=\mathrm{dim}(E)
>$$
### 2. Proof

>[!info] Proof
>Since $\mathrm{Ker}(f)$ is a *subspace* of $E$, one can find $S\subset E$ such that $\mathrm{Ker}(f)\ci S=E$.
