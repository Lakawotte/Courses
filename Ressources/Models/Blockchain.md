# Definition
## I. Statement
>[!hint] Preamble : Distributed Ledger
>A *distributed ledger* is a system where by replicated, shared, and synchronized digital data is geographically spread (distributed) across many sites, countries, or institutions. Its fundamental rationale is *argumentum ad populum* whereby its veracity relies on a popular or majority of nodes to force the system to agree.

In contrast to a *centralized database*, a distributed ledger does not require a central administrator, and consequently does not have a single (central) point-of-failure.
