# Application
## I. Meaning
**Year-over-year** is a method that consists in comparing a given statistic with the same stat one year earlier.
## II. Use
This method of calculation is preffered to monthly comparison which may be subject to seasonal trends.
It's a simple way to compare performances of portfolios or companies.
