# Formula
## I. Statement
### 1. Expression

>[!hint] Theorem
>Let  $(a,b)\in\mathbb{R}^2$ and  $f:[a;b]\rightarrow\mathbb{R}$ a **[[Continuity|continuous]]** function **[[Differentiability|differentiable]]** on $]a;b[$ such that $f(a)=f(b)$. Then,
>$$
\exists c\in]a;b[,f'(c)=0
>$$
