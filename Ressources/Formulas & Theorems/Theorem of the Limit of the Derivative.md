# Formula
## I. Statement
### 1. Expression

>[!hint] Theorem
>Let $I\subset\mathbb{R}$ and $a\in I$. Let $f:I\to\mathbb{R}$ be **[[Continuity|continuous]]** on $I$ and **[[Differentiability|differentiable]]** on $I\textbackslash\{x_0\}$ :
>- If $f'\xrightarrow[]{x\to a}l$ with $l\in\mathbb{R}$, then $f$ is **[[Differentiability|differentiable]]** at $a$ and $f'(a)=l$. Then $f'$ is **[[Continuity|continuous]]** on $a$.
>- If $f'\xrightarrow[]{x\to a}+\infty$, then the **[[Rate of Change|rate of change]]** $\frac{f(x)-f(a)}{x-a}$ is also diverging to $+\infty$.
