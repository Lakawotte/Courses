### 1. Corollaries

>[!tip] Difference of Derivatives
>>[!tldr] Theorem
>>Let $f$ and $g$ be two $\mathcal{D}^1$ functions from $\mathbb{R}$ to $I$.
>>$$
f'=g'\Longrightarrow\exists c\in\mathbb{R},f=g+c
>>$$
>
>>[!info] Proof
>>Let $F=f-g$, then $F'=0$ on $I$. By an extension of the **[[Mean Value Theorem|mean value theorem]]**, $F$ must be a constant.

> [!tip] Antiderivative
>>[!tldr] Theorem
>>If $F$ is an antiderivative of $f$ on an interval $I$, then all the antiderivatives are given by $F+c$ where $c$ is describing $\mathbb{R}$.
>
>>[!info] Proof
>>
